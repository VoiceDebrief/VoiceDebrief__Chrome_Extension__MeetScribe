Reviews of MeetScribe, and what happened to each finding

Every review of MeetScribe is kept here as it was received, with a status for every finding. A finding is fixed only when a test pins it; partly says which half is done; open is not done yet. Nothing is deleted when it is fixed.

The v0.3.1 review (ChatGPT, 3 November 2026)

The review as received (PDF, 11 pages). It was written by ChatGPT from the code at commit a4bbdca, without a real Meet call or paid API calls. Its verdict: keep this as a supervised beta; fix the privacy and data-integrity defects before widening distribution. We agree, and MeetScribe stays in beta.

Fixed in v0.3.2. The tests are in tests/unit.test.mjs and tests/extension.test.mjs, each named after its finding.

#PriorityFindingStatusWhat changed
01P1Deleting a running meeting does not stop its capturebuilt fixedDeletion goes through the service worker, which stops capture first. A segment can only be written into a meeting that exists, and a result arriving for a deleted meeting is discarded with its segment. The wording says a delete cannot recall audio already sent. Test: 01
02P1Buffered muted audio can be admitted after unmutebuilt fixedMute and pause are applied inside the worklet, on the audio thread. Gated samples are counted and dropped, never buffered, and the partial batch is posted before every change. The test is the mute test (no microphone segments while muted). A tone placed exactly at the boundary is not tested yet
03P2Privacy settings overstate their guaranteesbuilt fixedThe zero-data-retention option is described as a request applied under the OpenRouter account's own settings, not a guarantee. The site and the extension say not to use MeetScribe for confidential meetings during the beta, and that local data is not encrypted. "Delete immediately" now happens when a segment is transcribed, and the alarms are re-created at startup
04P1Stopping offline can block the next meeting indefinitelybuilt fixedRequests time out at 75 s. Stop sends for at most 20 s, then frees recording and hands the rest to the service worker, and the meeting says uploading. Test: 04
05P1Fixing a rejected key does not resume live transcriptionbuilt fixedSaving a key restarts the running capture's own drain, after waiting for the held one to wind down (the first fix raced with it, and the test caught it). Test: 05
06P2Failed startup and missing-recorder stops strand session statebuilt fixedStart, stop and delete run one at a time; any failure in start rolls back to idle with the reason. Stop without a capture page settles on its own; pause and mute change state only if the capture page answered. Test: 06
07P1Storage failures silently discard speechbuilt fixedA failed write pauses capture, records the lost span on the meeting, and the panel says what was not saved. The meeting ends complete-with-gaps. Test: 07 (a test-build switch makes storage fail)
08P1Empty transcription is hidden as successful completionbuilt fixedNo text for a segment that held speech is a new status, empty: shown as a gap, audio kept, retryable, its cost counted, named in the debrief's input. Test: 08
09P2The echo heuristic removes legitimate replies from debriefsbuilt fixedProbable echoes stay in what the debrief model reads, marked as uncertain; the prompt says a marked line may be a genuine reply. Test: finding 09 (unit)
10P1A debrief can be saved into the wrong meetingbuilt fixedThe meeting is fixed before anything is awaited, and the debrief is written only to it, only if it still exists. Test: 10 (switches meeting while the reply is delayed)
11P1The published quick start tests the key without saving itbuilt fixedOne button, Save and test, and the guides say so. Test: 11 follows the home page's steps to an enabled Start
12P2Meeting selection and status feedback are not accessiblespecified partlyMeetings are buttons with a focus outline; inputs and selects have labels; status lines are live regions; the page rebuilds only what changed, so focus survives. Not done: a screen-reader audit, and keyboard tests
13P2The lease cannot guarantee a segment is billed only oncespecified partlyThe lease (180 s) now outlives the longest request (75 s), so a live request cannot be claimed twice. A context that dies after sending can still cause a second send: the code and docs now say "at least once". Not done: an attempt ledger with uncertain spend
14P2Every HTTP 400 disables verbose timestamps for that modelbuilt fixedOnly a 400 that names the response format or timestamps switches a model to plain JSON. Test: 14
15P2Model timestamps are accepted outside the audio segmentbuilt fixedA timestamp must lie inside the audio sent (half a second of tolerance), or the response's times are set aside and its lines use the segment's start. Test: 15, and the capture test now checks the rule on real segments
16P2Fallback resampling drifts; Stop drops the worklet tailspecified partlyStop flushes the worklet and waits for its acknowledgement, so the tail is kept. Not done: a stateful resampler. It is only used if Chrome refuses a 16 kHz context, which it has not so far
17P2The shipped build identifies an older source commitunverified openThe stamp still names the commit before the release commit. Next: stamp in CI from the release checkout, and publish an artifact-to-commit manifest
18P2Debriefs can become stale without warning or verifiable citationsspecified partlyA debrief records the transcript it was written from, and says when the transcript has changed since. The page says its times are the model's references, not checked links. Not done: citations validated against line IDs and made navigable

Not acted on yet, from its wider assessment: the attempt ledger, indexed queries for long histories, branch protection on dev, an optional session-only key, and its QA plan for real devices (now the beta's job).

The first real call (Dinis Cruz, 3 November 2026)

S1 answered: no. Opening the side panel straight from the toolbar icon (route a in the plan) did not let Chrome capture the Meet tab. The panel showed "the active tab", because it could not even read the tab's title. Its error flashed and vanished, wiped by the panel's refresh.

Fixed in v0.3.2 with route b. The icon click is handled by MeetScribe itself, which counts as invoking the extension on that tab. It opens the panel and remembers the tab, and Start asks Chrome for that tab's stream. The panel names the tab it will capture, or says to click the icon on the Meet tab; an error stays until the next Start. Automation cannot click the toolbar, so the test pins the rest of the route (no tab chosen: Start disabled, a clear message; a tab that was not clicked on is refused), and the real check is the next call.

The second real call (Dinis Cruz, 3 November 2026)

S1 confirmed: yes. With v0.3.2's route the Meet tab was captured and transcribed, fourteen minutes and more, lines arriving as the call went on.

S2, open: no "You" lines appeared. The microphone was ticked, but none of the speaker's own words were transcribed. The likely causes are the microphone never being granted on the options page, or the capture page's request failing. The panel showed the reason only in small text, under the meters. v0.3.3 makes it impossible to miss. Ticking the microphone before it is allowed shows a red notice with an Allow the microphone button. If the microphone fails at Start, a red banner stays for the whole meeting, naming Chrome's reason and saying the call is still being transcribed. The next call answers S2. v0.3.4 goes further, at the editor's request: a strip at the top of the panel shows the microphone's state at all times, red whenever your voice is not or would not be transcribed.

The "Meeting:" label on every line was noise (the editor's words: redundant). From v0.3.3 only You is labelled, on screen and in exports; the debrief's input keeps both labels.

VoiceDebrief MeetScribe

The VoiceDebrief transcript and debrief, for the Google Meet call you are in: a Chrome extension on your own OpenRouter key, with no bot in the call, no Google recording and no server of ours. Beta. Content CC BY 4.0; code Apache 2.0.

Your audio and its transcript are sent to OpenRouter, which passes them to a model provider. We do not know which provider handles your recording, and we have no control over what they do with it. This site is static and has no analytics.
