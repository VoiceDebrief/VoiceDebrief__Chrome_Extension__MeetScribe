Release history

One version for the site and the extension, in admin/build/version.txt. Every push to dev is a release: CI validates, runs the tests, tags vX.Y.Z on the commit whose subject carries it (site vX.Y.Z: ...), then publishes the site.

VersionDateWhat changed
v0.3.13 November 2026The site moves to its domain, meetscribe.chrome-extension.voicedebrief.ai (brief v2.0, open question 1, answered): canonical links, og:url, sitemap.xml, robots.txt and absolute URLs in llms.txt, all built from the CNAME file; the extension's homepage points at it; the gate checks they agree. No change to how the extension behaves.
v0.3.03 November 2026Aligned with voicedebrief.ai: its colour schemes (all five, chosen on the options page, and the gate refuses colour outside them), its brand and BETA mark, its sentences on where audio goes and on model transcripts, its key guidance. The debrief: voicedebrief.ai's summary prompt made for a meeting, in its four locales, with google/gemini-3.5-flash, every point timed back to its line; in the exports. Build, test and release and MeetScribe and VoiceDebrief pages. The test runner asks for TAP by name (Node 24 on CI changed the default reporter). 44 tests.
v0.2.03 November 2026The first MVP of the extension: tab capture played back on your device, an opt-in microphone with its own mute, 16 kHz taps cut at pauses, every segment checked and persisted before it is sent, the OpenRouter queue with leases, retries and the key check, the side panel, options and meetings pages, exports. 38 tests (Node and Chromium). CI now validates, tests, tags and deploys. Site: install, what is built, this page.
v0.1.03 November 2026The site: the review of brief v1.0, brief v2.0, the plan, the models snapshot, the sources. No extension.
VoiceDebrief MeetScribe

The VoiceDebrief transcript and debrief, for the Google Meet call you are in: a Chrome extension on your own OpenRouter key, with no bot in the call, no Google recording and no server of ours. Beta. Content CC BY 4.0; code Apache 2.0.

Your audio and its transcript are sent to OpenRouter, which passes them to a model provider. We do not know which provider handles your recording, and we have no control over what they do with it. This site is static and has no analytics.
