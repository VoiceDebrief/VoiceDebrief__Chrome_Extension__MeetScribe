Release history

One version for the site and the extension, in admin/build/version.txt. A push to dev is a minor release (v0.3.1 → v0.3.2); only a push to main is a major one (v0.4.0). CI validates, runs the tests, tags vX.Y.Z on the commit whose subject carries it (site vX.Y.Z: ...), then publishes the site from dev.

v0.3.43 November 2026The microphone's state is impossible to miss (the editor: "make it real obvious when that is not set"). A strip at the top of the side panel, in every state: red when your voice is not or would not be transcribed (not allowed, blocked, failed), with the button that fixes it; amber when it is off for the meeting or muted; green when your voice is being transcribed. Only "You" is labelled: "Meeting:" on every line was redundant. 63 tests.
v0.3.23 November 2026Capture works the way Chrome allows (the first real call): the icon click is handled by MeetScribe, which opens the panel and remembers the tab; Start captures that tab; errors stay on screen. 13 of the v0.3.1 review's 18 findings fixed, 4 more in part, 1 open, each pinned by a test: deletion stops capture (01), mute applied on the audio thread (02), honest privacy wording (03), Stop no longer blocks on a dead network (04), a fixed key resumes a live recording (05), startup rollback (06), storage failures surfaced (07), empty transcriptions are gaps (08), echoes kept in the debrief's input (09), the debrief stays with its meeting (10), Save and test (11), labelled controls (12, in part). Privacy, stated plainly: client-side on our side only; once audio reaches OpenRouter we cannot see or verify what happens to it; not for confidential meetings during the beta. 61 tests.

VoiceDebrief MeetScribe

The VoiceDebrief transcript and debrief, for the Google Meet call you are in: a Chrome extension on your own OpenRouter key, with no bot in the call, no Google recording and no server of ours. Beta. Content CC BY 4.0; code Apache 2.0.

Your audio and its transcript are sent to OpenRouter, which passes them to a model provider. We do not know which provider handles your recording, and we have no control over what they do with it. This site is static and has no analytics.
