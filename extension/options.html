Meetings

1. Your OpenRouter key

MeetScribe, like voicedebrief.ai, runs on your own key: we charge nothing and add no margin, and you pay OpenRouter directly. Use a key with a credit limit: that limit is the real spending cap. The key is kept in this browser profile and sent only to openrouter.ai. How do I get a key? Five steps, and how to cap what it can spend.

2. Microphone

Needed only if you want your own voice transcribed (labelled You). Chrome asks once, here; the capture page cannot ask, because it is never shown.

3. Where you hear the meeting

Capturing a tab silences it, so MeetScribe plays it back. Pick the device you use for Meet, or the meeting may move to another speaker when capture starts.

4. Transcription

Recommended models first, with their price per hour of audio and what our test showed; then every transcription model OpenRouter lists now.

Struggling with fast talkers? Our test on synthetic speech could not tell the good models apart, so try them on your own audio: on the meetings page, ⇄ on any line transcribes that piece again with another model, side by side, and Transcribe again with redoes a whole meeting.

Your audio and its transcript are sent to OpenRouter, which passes them to a model provider. We do not know which provider handles your recording, and we have no control over what they do with it. Do not use this for anything you would not hand to a third party.

Beta: do not transcribe confidential meetings. On MeetScribe's side everything is client-side, and nothing goes to a server of ours. Once audio leaves this browser for OpenRouter, we cannot see or verify who processes it or whether it is kept.

5. The debrief

After a meeting, MeetScribe can write the debrief: key points, decisions, actions, open questions, one sentence. It uses the same structure, language and tone settings as voicedebrief.ai. The transcript is never changed; the debrief is a separate document.

Recommended models first, with an estimate for an hour of meeting (about 17,000 tokens in, 1,000 out); then every text model OpenRouter lists now. You can also pick a model each time you write a debrief.
